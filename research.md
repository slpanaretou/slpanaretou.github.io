### Hedley Single Molecule Laboratory
**School of Chemistry, University of Glasgow | May–September 2026**

Worked as a summer intern in the Hedley Single Molecule Laboratory, contributing to experimental, computational, and instrumentation-focused research in single-molecule spectroscopy.

My work included:

- Electromechanical design of photonic components.
- Laser safety certification.
- Preparation of spectroscopic slides under clean-room conditions.
- Performing and analysing single-molecule spectroscopy (SMS) measurements on materials synthesised by collaborating research groups.
- Developing image-processing features for the Hedley Lab Confocal (HLC) software to improve measurement precision.
- Developing deconvolution methods for photon-measurement data.

**Technical experience:** Python · CAD · VHDL
